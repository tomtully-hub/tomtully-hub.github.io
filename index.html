Tom Research Index

A private, owner-operated tool that keeps an inventory of research files in the owner's Google Drive and records metadata changes for reconciliation on the owner's Mac.

The application reads file metadata, creates its own inventory outputs, and runs scheduled checks. It does not request permission to download the contents of every Drive file. It has no public registration or public data endpoint.

The owner grants access through Google and can revoke it in Google Account connections.
